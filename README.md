# RevGrowth

RevGrowth is a financial and growth performance platform designed to solve the **Two-Half Problem**.

## The Two-Half Problem
Most SaaS companies operate with two disconnected halves:
1. **The Acquisition / Growth Half**: Top-of-funnel marketing, campaigns, traffic, and signup conversion.
2. **The Revenue / Retention Half**: Downstream churn, LTV, net revenue retention (NRR), and unit economics.

When these two halves don't communicate, acquisition budget is spent on high-churn customer segments while profitable retention opportunities are overlooked. RevGrowth bridges the gap by linking top-of-funnel campaign data directly to long-term cohort revenue retention.
